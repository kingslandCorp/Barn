Pool safety

There is no lifeguard. The pool is used at your own risk. Please supervise your group at all times.

  • Watch children closely; never leave children alone near water.
  • No diving or jumping.
  • Never swim alone.
  • No glass by the pool — use plastic tumblers.
  • Bring your own pool towels and flip-flops.
  • Do not swim after alcohol, if unwell, or if drowsy.
  • In an emergency dial 999. Life ring and reaching pole are poolside; first aid kit is in the kitchen.

Before you leave

Return keys to the lockbox, run the dishwasher, switch off lights, close windows and doors, roll the pool cover back over the water, leave towels in the bath or laundry basket, empty bins into the correct bags, and do a final sweep for chargers and valuables.
